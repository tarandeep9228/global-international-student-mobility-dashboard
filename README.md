🌍 Global International Student Mobility Analysis (2015–2024)
Overview
This Power BI dashboard explores international student mobility trends between 2015 and 2024.

Dashboard Highlights
📈 31 Million Students Analyzed
🌎 60 Destination Countries
🏠 10 Origin Countries
📅 10 Years of Data (2015–2024)
Key Insights
International student mobility increased from 2.5M in 2015 to 3.6M in 2024.
USA is the leading destination country.
India and China are the largest source countries.
Student distribution between male and female populations is relatively balanced.
